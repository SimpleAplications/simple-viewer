Simple

A simple picture and video viewer for your private use. It never connects to the internet: no telemetry, no account, no ads.

Download for Windows

Free · Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit · open source
Portable zip (no install) · Checksums · All versions

What it does

One frame at a time, both ways

Tap X or Z to move exactly one frame forward or back. Hold to keep going, faster the longer you hold. Every frame is shown complete, with no smearing after a jump.

Pictures, GIFs and video together

Open one file and move through everything else in the folder with the arrow keys. Zoom with the mouse wheel, drag to pan, rotate and flip.

Stays on your PC

The app has no network code and keeps no history of what you open. The only thing it saves is your own options: keys, window colour and playback choices.

Picture controls

Brightness, contrast, saturation, gamma and warmth for anything on screen, including paused video. Display only: your files are never changed.

Your keys, your colour

Choose the keys for play, next, previous and frame stepping, and pick the window colour. Text switches between white and black to stay readable.

Play a whole folder

Let it move to the next file when a video ends, in order or shuffled, once or on repeat. Or repeat a single video.

Formats

PicturesJPG, PNG, BMP, TIFF, WebP, AVIF, HEIC, JPEG XL, JPEG 2000, TGA, QOI, ICO, SVG
AnimationsGIF, animated PNG, animated WebP
VideoMP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, AVI, WMV, FLV, MPEG, TS and more, with H.264, H.265, VP9, AV1 and other codecs
DecodingOn the graphics card where it supports the format, otherwise on the processor

Installing

Windows may warn you the first time. The installer is not digitally signed yet, so Windows can show “Windows protected your PC”. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

To check that your download is genuine, compare its SHA-256 checksum with SHA256SUMS.txt. In PowerShell: Get-FileHash Simple-Setup.exe

The installer needs no administrator rights and can add Simple to Explorer’s “Open with” list. To update, run a newer installer over the old one.

Open source inside

Simple decodes pictures and video with libraries from the FFmpeg project, licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License. FFmpeg is included as separate, unmodified DLL files that you may replace with your own build. The FFmpeg source code for the included build is published with every release, and the full notices are in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt inside the app’s folder.
