The app has a new name: **Reiks Viewer**, by Reiks Apps. Earlier versions were called Simple. Nothing about how it works has changed.

"Reiks" is the Gothic word for a ruler, pronounced "reeks".

## Download

- **ReiksViewer-Setup.exe**: the installer. No administrator rights needed.
- **ReiksViewer-portable.zip**: no install; unzip anywhere and run `ReiksViewer.exe`.

The files with `1.1.0` in the name are the same downloads under their versioned names.

Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the installer is not digitally signed yet: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. To check your download, compare its SHA-256 with `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## Coming from Simple

- **Reiks Viewer installs alongside Simple, not over it.** Uninstall Simple from Windows' "Installed apps" when you no longer want it.
- **Your options carry over.** Keys, window colour, playback and privacy choices are read from Simple's options file the first time, and saved from then on in `%APPDATA%\ReiksViewer\settings.json`.
- **"Open with" and default-app choices** made for Simple don't transfer; tick those options again in the new installer if you use them.
