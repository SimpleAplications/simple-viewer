Reiks Viewer gets ready for the Microsoft Store, and the website gets a privacy policy.

## Download

- **ReiksViewer-Setup-1.5.0.exe**: the installer. Installing for yourself needs no administrator rights.
- **ReiksViewer-1.5.0-portable.zip**: no install; unzip anywhere and run `ReiksViewer.exe`.

`ReiksViewer-Setup.exe` and `ReiksViewer-portable.zip` are the same downloads without the version in the name. To update, run the new installer over the old one.

Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the installer is not digitally signed yet: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. To check your download, compare its SHA-256 with `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## New

- **A privacy policy** on the website, in English, Spanish and Swedish: what the app keeps on your computer, what goes over the network, and what changes when it is installed from the Microsoft Store.
- **Ready for the Microsoft Store.** When Reiks Viewer runs from its Store package, "Check for updates" opens its page in the Store, which installs its updates, and "Open codecs folder" opens a folder where you can put your own build of FFmpeg, because the Store installs apps in a folder nobody may change. The installer and the zip work as before.

## FFmpeg

Reiks Viewer uses libraries from the [FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org) project under the LGPL, shipped as separate, unmodified DLLs. `ffmpeg-source-n8.1.3-17-gc389840894.tar.gz` is the FFmpeg source code of the included build. See `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt` in the app's folder.
