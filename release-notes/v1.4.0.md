Reiks Viewer can print.

## Download

- **ReiksViewer-Setup-1.4.0.exe**: the installer. Installing for yourself needs no administrator rights.
- **ReiksViewer-1.4.0-portable.zip**: no install; unzip anywhere and run `ReiksViewer.exe`.

`ReiksViewer-Setup.exe` and `ReiksViewer-portable.zip` are the same downloads without the version in the name. To update, run the new installer over the old one.

Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the installer is not digitally signed yet: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. To check your download, compare its SHA-256 with `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## New

- **Print.** `Ctrl` + `P`, or the Print button in the new menu, opens a print view inside the window. It shows the page as it will come out, and lets you choose the printer, the paper size, the orientation, whether the whole picture is shown or the page is filled, and the number of copies. "Printer settings…" opens the printer's own settings, for paper type and quality. The picture is printed as it is shown, with its rotation, flips and image settings. For a video, the frame on screen is printed. With "Hide file names" on, the print job is not named after the file.

## Changed

- **A menu for playback and print** (the three dots). "Play the whole folder" with its options and "Repeat the current file" have moved there from Options, next to Print. The button stays lit while a folder is being played, and `S` still switches that on and off.
- **Options** now holds only what belongs to the app itself: keys, privacy, window colour and language.

## Good to know

Printing goes through Windows' own print system. Windows and the printer's driver talk to the printer, over your network if it is a network printer, and they may keep a record of the job. Reiks Viewer's own code still has no networking.

The printer, paper size and size choice you last printed with are kept in the options file, next to your other options.

## FFmpeg

Reiks Viewer uses libraries from the [FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org) project under the LGPL, shipped as separate, unmodified DLLs. `ffmpeg-source-n8.1.3-17-gc389840894.tar.gz` is the FFmpeg source code of the included build. See `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt` in the app's folder.
