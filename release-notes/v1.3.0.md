Two menus instead of three, and they close when you click away.

## Download

- **ReiksViewer-Setup-1.3.0.exe**: the installer. Installing for yourself needs no administrator rights.
- **ReiksViewer-1.3.0-portable.zip**: no install; unzip anywhere and run `ReiksViewer.exe`.

`ReiksViewer-Setup.exe` and `ReiksViewer-portable.zip` are the same downloads without the version in the name. To update, run the new installer over the old one.

Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the installer is not digitally signed yet: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. To check your download, compare its SHA-256 with `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## Changed

- **Image settings is one menu.** Brightness, contrast, saturation, gamma and warmth now share a menu with rotate and flip. Its button is a picture with a small gear, and `A` opens it. The separate sun and rotate buttons are gone.
- **Options holds everything about the app itself**, and the window colour is back there, next to playback, keys, privacy and language.
- **Clicking outside an open menu closes it.** So does `Esc`. On the picture, that click only closes the menu.

## Installing over an older version

- **Replaces Simple.** Installing Reiks Viewer now removes Simple (the app's earlier name) instead of sitting beside it: your options are kept, and if Simple was your default app, that choice carries over to Reiks Viewer. A copy of Simple installed for all users is only removed when Reiks Viewer is also installed for all users.
- **Default apps.** The installer now adds Reiks Viewer to Windows' default apps list together with "Open with", so it can be chosen in Settings later without reinstalling, and it tells Explorer about the change at once. "Default app settings" in the app (F1) opens the right Settings page when the app is installed for all users.
- **The version is easier to see.** The website names the current version and downloads the file with the version in its name, and the installer file's properties in Windows show its version.
