## English (en-us)

**Short description**

A private picture and video viewer. It never connects to the internet, keeps no history and never changes your files.

**Description**

```
Reiks Viewer shows your pictures, GIFs and videos, and that is all it does. It is made for private use.

WHAT IT NEVER DOES
• Connect to the internet. Its own code has no networking: no telemetry, no usage statistics, no crash reports of its own, no update checks. Its decoders are limited to local files.
• Keep a record of what you open. No history, no recent list, no thumbnail cache, no library of your folders.
• Change your files. It never writes to your pictures, videos or their folders.
• Run in the background. No service, no startup entry, no tray icon.
• Ask for anything. No account, no sign-in, no ads.

WHAT IT DOES
• Pictures, GIFs and video in one window. Open one file and move through the rest of the folder with the arrow keys. Zoom, pan, rotate and flip.
• One frame at a time, both ways. Step exactly one frame forward or back, with the time shown to the millisecond.
• Image settings: brightness, contrast, saturation, gamma and warmth, for display only.
• Private on screen too. Have Windows leave the window out of screenshots, recordings and screen sharing, hide file names, and blank the window with one key.
• Print a picture, or the frame of a paused video, and see the page before it is sent.
• Play a whole folder, in order or shuffled, or repeat one video.
• Your own keys and window colour. Seven languages.

FORMATS
Pictures: JPG, PNG, BMP, TIFF, WebP, AVIF, HEIC, JPEG XL, JPEG 2000, TGA, QOI, ICO, SVG. Animations: GIF, animated PNG, animated WebP. Video: MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, AVI, WMV, FLV, MPEG, TS and more. Whether a particular file opens depends on what is inside it.

GOOD TO KNOW
"Hide from screenshots" relies on a Windows feature: it cannot stop a camera pointed at your screen or software made to get around it. Files on network drives or in cloud-synced folders are moved by Windows or your sync program, and printing goes through Windows' own print system. The Microsoft Store installs the app's updates.

Reiks Viewer is free and open source. Pictures and video are decoded with libraries from the FFmpeg project, under the LGPL. Source code: github.com/reiks-apps/reiks-viewer
```
